Hi, I'm Kobe.

I'm a frontend developer and UI designer based in Edmonton, Alberta.

I design and build clear web experiences that help organizations share their brand and marketing stories with their communities.

I currently work in a cross-functional team at Digital + Creative (University of Alberta) designing and maintaining large institutional websites to support marketing + external relations needs.
