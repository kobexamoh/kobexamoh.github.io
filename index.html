Hi there, I'm Kobe. I'm a frontend engineer-who-designs (or designer-who-develops) based out of Edmonton, Alberta.

My strengths are that of a design engineer - namely, rapid prototyping in the browser with semantic HTML, JS, and CSS frameworks like Tailwind CSS.

I'm comfortable performing UX research and user testing. I've used tools like WordPress and Squarespace to help small businesses and teams find creative solutions, and I use data from a variety of sources to make digital strategy recommendations.

I like clean typography, the Swiss design style, a good white chocolate mocha, and designing digital experiences that simplify information for users.
